Clarity and transparency before we begin.

My goal is never to replace your voice — it's to capture it so faithfully that readers feel they're hearing directly from you. I spend countless hours listening to interviews and reading transcripts, identifying recurring themes and getting to know not just what you think, but how you think.

One of the comments I hear most often is, "It feels like I wrote this book myself."

From our first conversation to a completed manuscript, most projects take between eight and twelve months, depending on the complexity of the book and your schedule. Occasionally a shorter project comes together much more quickly — I once completed a shorter manuscript in three months, published a month later. The exception, not the rule, but proof it can move quickly when circumstances allow.

It's yours. The book is built around your ideas, your experiences and your voice, and by the time the manuscript is finished, it truly belongs to you.

I work with complete confidentiality for private, family stories, and every conversation is treated accordingly. For public stories, we can also discuss whether and how my involvement is credited.
