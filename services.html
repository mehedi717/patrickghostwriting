Services

Every book is different, and every author brings a unique story, voice and vision. I don't believe in a one-size-fits-all approach to ghostwriting.

01

Memoirs & Family Histories

Told for the enjoyment of family and friends, these projects are handled with complete confidentiality. Through recorded interviews, I capture the stories, characters and voice that deserve to be preserved — without the process ever being about anyone but you.

Ideal For:

Families and individuals preserving a life story privately, for the people who matter most.

02

True Stories, Told Publicly

For stories meant for a wider audience, I help bring your book to life from the first interview through to a finished manuscript — and can assist in securing publishing once it's ready.

Ideal For:

Authors with an extraordinary true story they want to share with the world.

03

Film & Screen Adaptation

Some true stories have the shape of a film as much as a book. Where a story has that kind of potential, I can help explore and arrange a possible film adaptation.

Ideal For:

Stories with cinematic potential, ready to be explored beyond the page.

04

Articles & Shorter Projects

Not every project is a book. For articles, blog posts and shorter publications, work is typically billed hourly — covering the interviews, planning, research and conversations behind the writing, not just the writing itself.

Ideal For:

Shorter pieces that still deserve a professional hand.
